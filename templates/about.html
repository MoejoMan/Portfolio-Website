About Me

My name is Joseph Hayes Gonzalez. Computers have always fascinated me, and somewhere along the way that curiosity turned into a passion for cybersecurity. I sit on the blue team side of things mostly, figuring out how to keep systems safe, building practical tools, and spreading awareness about safe internet practices.

I learn by doing. From turning a Raspberry Pi Pico into a USB Rubber Ducky, to building an SSH brute force analyser, to running a Cowrie honeypot that feeds an ELK SIEM and a self hosted WireGuard VPN on a Hetzner VPS, I am always looking for the next thing to build or break (responsibly). I run a homelab out of my uni dorm with Cisco switches, a refurbished Dell OptiPlex server, and cloud VPS instances.

In 2026 I worked as a DevOps Engineer at Netservers on Cambridge Fibre Networks projects, rebuilding an Odoo backend in Python and building a live broadband ordering platform with the CEO. You can read about it in the case study. On my degree I have investigated a simulated extortion case end to end, from evidence handling to giving expert evidence in a mock trial.

I am looking for a part time role now and a graduate role from summer 2027, in a SOC, a digital forensics team, a security consultancy, or anywhere I can help defend critical infrastructure.
